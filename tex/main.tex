\begin{abstract}
We prove that in every family of abelian varieties of Weil type, for any CM
field and any dimension, the Weil classes are algebraic on a dense set of
members, which contains an explicit member where they are polynomials in
divisor classes and is either the whole family or meagre.
\end{abstract}
